## 4. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Brand Authority

Search engines and LLM-powered answer systems (Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Bing Copilot) evaluate credibility based on consistent entity identification, structured data, and readable content:

1. **Clear Entity Definition:** Vyrobytex is identified consistently as a digital solutions agency founded by Navin Chaudhary, providing 4 core services.
2. **Accessible DOM Hierarchy:** Core business data, service offerings, and capabilities are rendered directly in semantic HTML elements, accessible to crawlers without requiring client interaction or form submission.
3. **Verified Social Profiles (`sameAs`):** Links strictly to genuine business profiles (Instagram, WhatsApp, GitHub).
4. **Structured JSON-LD Graph:** Every public page includes valid schema (`Organization`, `WebSite`, `Service`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage`, `CreativeWork`) matching visible on-page content.
